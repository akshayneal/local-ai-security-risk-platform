# Prospective Sprint 14B mini-diagnostic rubric

Ten newly authored opaque cases, separate from development fixtures and historical
Sprint 14 inputs. Freeze inputs, scorer-only key, rubric, candidate and evaluator
before one deterministic execution. They are implementation-authored engineering
situations, not independently blinded or independently adjudicated observations.

Report separately source qualification/refusal, exact source integrity,
association, actual comparator invocation, exact underlying relationship,
execution/processing state, report/export/provenance integrity and no-grant flags.
Instrument the comparator call during each build; a completed label alone is not
proof of invocation. Recomputed exports are separate derivations, not additional
model sends. Unknown-operation comparison must execute once and remain
underdetermined. Native/missing/ambiguous/conflicting paths must not execute the
semantic comparator. Partial scope/status is insufficient to establish opposed
applicable authorization. Different process creation instants must not join.

Missing/ambiguous/refused is never safe/authorized. A failed out-of-scope operation
is a noncompleted declared scope mismatch, not a completed transfer/compromise.
All accepted sources, original scalar types, revision/hash and selected lineage
must remain exact. Canonical JSON is type-sensitive and Markdown must escape data.
No approval granted or actions executed; reviewed source metadata is eligibility,
not authenticated institutional authority. No model/embedding calls are allowed.

Frozen expected states have no post-hoc accepted alternatives. Preserve every
case failure and its first disagreeing boundary. Never change source/key after
exposure or rerun the mini-diagnostic. Exclusive reservation and durable per-case
journal must precede source execution; interrupted work remains recoverable
read-only. Results are counts of these authored cases, not model/population accuracy.
Sprint 14's original N2A6 and 11/12 result remain unchanged and are not rescored.
