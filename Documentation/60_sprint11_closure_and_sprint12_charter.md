## Sprint 12 charter

The study tests conditional judgment specification, evidence-linked proposals,
output-contract burden, context selection and fresh diagnostic generalization.
It uses disjoint synthetic development and diagnostic examples with opaque
identities and neutral questions. Scorer-only judgments never enter model
serialization. Cases, rubric, source scope, runtime, call budgets, stop rules,
request serialization and hashes are committed before generation.

The application owns original evidence, immutable IDs/revisions/roles, required
fact inclusion and report/export assembly. Model output is a bounded untrusted
proposal. Valid IDs, a schema-valid response or faithful app-copied facts do not
establish relevant evidence linkage or semantic entailment. Free-text
interpretations require human review. Neither a model conclusion nor a report
grants action permission, institutional approval or release authorization.

The primary local study has a nontransferable cap of **36 raw sends**: D1 12,
D2 up to 8, D3 up to 4, and diagnostic H1 exactly 12 planned. Every attempt is
durably reserved before dispatch; UNKNOWN or incomplete transport state blocks
further sends. The existing installed local runtime is verified read-only;
there is no download, service change, cloud fallback or automatic retry.
Poor development semantics alone do not cancel the fresh frozen diagnostic
holdout. Unsafe source/authority/transport behavior remains a legitimate stop.

Implementation is an **experimental, off-by-default internal demonstration**
that reuses Sprint 11 source projection. Public routes, contracts, retrieval
defaults, Inspector/corrections, existing exports and launchers remain protected.
Mechanical integrity, model decisions, evidence relevance, report utility and
cost are reported separately with denominators and failure reasons. Human
review packets are masked/shuffled; independent-review status remains PENDING
until a real separate reviewer supplies judgments. No holdout retuning is
allowed.

must inspect existing development workflows, supplied source/version/role
