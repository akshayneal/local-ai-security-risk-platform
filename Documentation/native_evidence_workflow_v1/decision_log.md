## 2 — Native core semantics and preservation before implementation (2026-11-07)

Earliest boundary: an uploaded vendor label is an observation, not an approved
source or completed-action predicate. Competing designs were (a) synthesize an
approved SourceReview to reuse authorization code, or (b) construct an explicitly
unreviewed upload snapshot with useful typed shapes and unsupported semantics.
Choose B; source IDs/revisions are content-derived locators, not authentication.

Discriminators: keep the exact ActionType while changing ResultType, replacing
Timestamp with naive/missing data, reusing PID at another creation instant,
swapping local/remote fields, adding permission claims and duplicate rows. None
may dispatch a comparator, grant authority or imply a completed connection. Typed
shape validation is separate from source truth; retain every original byte/value
and flag source-body authority claims as inert. Request/report/export fingerprints
bind selection/context and reject type-sensitive mutations. Existing parsers are
reused unchanged; no review flags or original v1/v2 bytes are modified.
