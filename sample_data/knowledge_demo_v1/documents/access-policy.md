# SYNTHETIC Northstar access and incident policy v2
Owner: Northstar incident lead. Scope: learning unit, catalog and internal RAG service. Requirement: incident containment affecting the catalog must have incident-lead approval recorded before execution. Preserve event identity and record the approval reference. Escalate uncertain cases for human review. This requirement does not establish that approval was granted.

For the internal RAG assistant, require learning-unit document eligibility before retrieval, separate policy requirements from implementation evidence, and review answers before operational use. Deployment approval and retention duration are not supplied by this policy.
